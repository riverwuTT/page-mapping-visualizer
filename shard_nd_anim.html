ND sharding

Arbitrary N-D sharding via TensorSpec::sharded(NdShardSpec, ShardShapeAlignment). Pad the logical tensor, align the shard shape, cut an N-D page grid into N-D shards (no flatten), then distribute shards to cores (round_robin or grid_2d). Default demo: logical [2,8,8] TILE 2×2, shard [2,4,4] on a 2×2 core grid → 4 shards of page-shape [2,2,2] (3D cubes).
